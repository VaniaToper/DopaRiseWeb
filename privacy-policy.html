DopaRise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DopaRise (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the DopaRise mobile app and doparise.com.

1. Who we are

DopaRise is a habit-tracking app. You can log daily habits, follow a personal score and streak, take an onboarding assessment, connect with other users, and receive optional reminders.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data. If you create an account: email address, password (stored by our authentication provider, not in readable form), and username.
  • Profile and social data. Username, optional avatar URL and bio, whether your profile is public, follow relationships, buddy (accountability) contracts, and activity you share with people in your network (for example streak or habit events).
  • Habit and progress data. Daily check-ins, scores, streaks, onboarding answers, reading progress, and app settings. This is stored on your device. Some profile stats (such as streak) may also be stored in our cloud database so they can appear on your profile.
  • Notifications. If you enable reminders, we store your notification preferences. Reminders are scheduled on your device. We do not send remote push notifications (no FCM or APNs).
  • Technical data. The app checks network connectivity to talk to our servers. Timezone is used only to schedule local reminders.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos from your camera roll (unless you later add that yourself), payment card details, or advertising identifiers. We do not run third-party analytics or ads SDKs.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account, and reset your password.
  • Provide habit tracking, scores, streaks, and insights.
  • Power social features (search, follow, buddies, activity).
  • Schedule optional on-device reminders you turn on.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the app and website.

4. Where data is stored

Account, profile, social, and in-app notification data are stored with Supabase, our authentication and database provider. Habit check-ins and most progress history stay in local storage on your device unless a feature needs a cloud copy for your profile or network.

Password-reset emails use a link on this website (doparise.com/reset-password).

5. Third-party services

We use a small set of processors to run the app:

  • Supabase — account authentication and cloud database.
  • Google Fonts — typography in the app.
  • Google Books API — book search when you look up a book to track reading. Your search query is sent to Google for that purpose.

Images (avatars, book covers) may be cached on your device to load faster. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the processors listed above, to operate the service.
  • With other DopaRise users, to the extent your profile is public or you use social features (follow, buddies, activity).
  • If required by law, or to protect the service and its users.

7. Permissions

DopaRise may ask for notification permission so it can remind you to log habits. You can deny or later turn this off in system settings and in the app. Vibration may be used for haptic feedback if you leave that setting on.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep account and cloud data while your account exists. Local habit data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

In the app you can: sign out; reset today’s check-in; or reset all local app data (Settings). To delete your account and associated cloud data, contact us using the details below and we will process the request.

9. Children

DopaRise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us. You can also make your profile private in Settings so it is hidden from username search.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. We use them only as needed to provide the app.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of DopaRise after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your account: use Settings → Give feedback in the DopaRise app, or visit doparise.com.
