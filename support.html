DopaRise is a habit-tracking app. You can log daily habits, follow a personal score and streak, take an onboarding assessment, connect with other users, and receive optional reminders on your device.

Getting started

After install, DopaRise asks an onboarding assessment (sleep, screen time, reading, smoking, and similar lifestyle questions). From your answers it sets a starting level and a small set of focus habits.

You can use the app without an account; check-ins then stay only on this device. Creating an account (email and username) unlocks social features (search, follows, buddies) and syncs your progress to the cloud so you can restore it after reinstall.

Habits you can track

Daily check-in covers these habits:

  • Exercise, sleep, water, meditation, reading
  • Cold shower, sunlight, screen time
  • Smoke-free, alcohol, junk food

Completing habits updates your score and streak. Without an account, check-ins stay on this device. With an account, they also sync to the cloud. You can reset today’s check-in or retake the onboarding test from Settings without losing your overall history, unless you choose Reset all app data (that also wipes the cloud copy when you are signed in).

Account and password

  • Forgot your password? Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. We send a reset link to doparise.com/reset-password.
  • Signed in? Change your password in Settings → Change password.
  • Sign out keeps local habit data on the device. Reset all app data clears local progress, deletes the cloud copy for that account, and returns the app to first launch.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. DopaRise does not send remote push notifications. If reminders do not appear:

  • Allow notifications for DopaRise in iOS or Android settings.
  • Turn the relevant reminders on in the app’s Notification settings.
  • Do not force-quit the app if you want scheduled reminders to fire.

Social features

  • Public profile. When this is on, others can find you by username search. Turn it off in Settings to hide from search.
  • Buddies. You can have up to three accountability partners. Invite them from Settings → Buddies.
  • Activity in your network can include streak milestones and that you completed certain habits.

Reading tracker

You can log reading time and optional book progress (title and pages) in the app. That data stays on the device, and syncs with your other progress if you have an account. DopaRise does not search an external books API.

Delete your account

To delete your account and associated cloud data, email ivan.hodynchuk77@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will process the request. You can also clear local data in Settings → Reset all app data, or by uninstalling the app.
